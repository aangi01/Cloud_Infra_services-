Q1. How large is the global cloud infrastructure market and how fast is it expected to grow?
The market was USD 254B in 2024 and is expected to reach USD 910B by 2034 (13.5% CAGR). Growth is driven by AI, digital transformation, and multi-cloud adoption.

Q2. What percentage of enterprises worldwide have migrated critical workloads to the cloud?
Over 70% of enterprises have migrated at least one critical workload. Cloud is now a core IT strategy for scalability and innovation.

Q3. What is the role of AI investments by hyperscalers in shaping cloud infrastructure?
Hyperscalers invested $150B+ in AI infrastructure like GPUs. This boosts demand for high-performance computing and storage.

Q4. How are sustainability and environmental concerns reshaping cloud infrastructure design?
Cloud design is evolving due to high energy usage concerns. Companies are adopting renewable energy and efficient cooling systems.

Q5. What are sovereign cloud deployments and why are they gaining traction in Europe?
Sovereign cloud keeps data within a region for legal compliance. It is growing in Europe due to strict GDPR regulations.

Q6. Which regions are leading cloud infrastructure adoption and which are growing the fastest?
North America leads adoption, while Asia-Pacific is growing fastest. Growth is driven by policies and government support.

Q7. How is the cloud infrastructure market structured in terms of hardware and software components?
It includes hardware (servers, storage) and software (services). Software-defined systems improve automation and reduce costs.

Q8. What is the significance of data security and compliance as a challenge for cloud infrastructure?
Security and compliance are major concerns in cloud adoption. Sensitive industries face strict regulations and cyber risks.

Q9. What role does cloud infrastructure play in enabling IoT and next-generation digital services?
Cloud provides scalable compute and storage for IoT. It enables real-time data processing and analytics.

Q10. How are regional cloud providers competing with global hyperscalers?
They focus on local compliance and data sovereignty. This attracts regulated industries and government sectors.

Q11. What are the key cloud technology trends identified for 2024 and beyond?
Key trends include AI, edge computing, multi-cloud, and better data management. Security and sustainability are also important.

Q12. What is an Industry Cloud Platform and how does it accelerate digital transformation?

Q13. How is Generative AI changing the way cloud services are designed and used?

Q17. How is cloud governance and compliance managed in large enterprises?

Q32. What is Disaster Recovery as a Service (DRaaS) and what does it include?
DRaaS is cloud-based disaster recovery. It includes replication, failover, and testing tools.

Q33. What is the difference between Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO)?
RTO is the time to restore systems. RPO is the acceptable data loss duration.

Q34. How does the Resource-Based View (RBV) theory explain cloud computing's role in organizational resilience?
RBV sees cloud as a valuable resource. It improves resilience and competitive advantage.

Q36. How does cloud DR compare to traditional on-premises disaster recovery in terms of cost and complexity?
Cloud DR is cheaper and simpler. It removes the need for backup data centers.

Q37. What are the four main disaster recovery strategies available in cloud computing?
Backup & Restore, Pilot Light, Warm Standby, Active-Active. They differ in cost and recovery speed.

Q38. How does geographic redundancy in cloud infrastructure protect against regional disasters?

Q39. Why is testing disaster recovery plans critical and how does cloud support DR testing?
